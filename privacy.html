Privacy Policy

Hermes · Effective 17 November 2026

Hermes is a private, single-user automation assistant operated by its owner for personal use. It is not offered to the public.

Google data Hermes may access

When the owner signs in with their Google account, Hermes may access the following data from that account only:

  • Gmail
  • Google Calendar
  • Google Docs
  • Google Sheets
  • Google Drive
  • Google Contacts

Hermes does not access Google data belonging to anyone other than the owner.

How that data is used

Google data is used only to perform tasks that the owner requests. It is not sold, is not shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising or for training AI models.

How that data is stored

Any Google data Hermes handles is stored only on the owner’s own machine, for the purpose of completing the requested task.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to revoke access

The owner can revoke Hermes’s access to their Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
